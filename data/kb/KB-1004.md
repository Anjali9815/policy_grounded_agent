### KB-1004 — Service-Outage Credits: Eligibility & Credit Calculation
**Category:** Service-Outage Credit Policy · **Last updated:** September 28, 2026

If Network Operations (NOC) confirms a service outage lasting **more than 4 consecutive hours** at your service address, you're eligible for a prorated **Service-Outage Credit**, calculated as:

```
(current monthly recurring charge ÷ 30) × number of full days without service
```

This credit applies to the recurring plan charge only and is issued **separately from, and is not limited by, the standard $75.00 goodwill credit ceiling described in KB-1002**. A larger outage can result in a credit above $75.00 without needing to fit inside that general ceiling.

To request this credit, you'll need the NOC outage ticket number (agents can look this up by service address and date range) and the request must be made within 30 days of the outage being resolved. See KB-1005 for the step-by-step request process.
