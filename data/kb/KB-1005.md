### KB-1005 — How to Request a Service-Outage Credit
**Category:** Service-Outage Credit Policy · **Last updated:** September 28, 2026

1. Confirm the customer's service address and the approximate outage dates.
2. Look up the NOC outage ticket (format: `OUT-#####`) covering that address and date range. If no ticket exists, the outage cannot be verified under this policy — route to KB-1002 instead.
3. Confirm the outage on the ticket lasted more than 4 consecutive hours.
4. Confirm the customer is requesting within **30 days of the ticket's resolution date**.
5. Calculate the credit: (monthly recurring charge ÷ 30) × full days affected.
6. If the calculated credit is $300.00 or less and the outage lasted 30 days or fewer, the agent may apply the credit directly. Above either threshold, escalate to a Tier 2 Billing Specialist before applying the credit.
