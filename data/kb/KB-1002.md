### KB-1002 — Refund Eligibility & Goodwill Credit Limits
**Category:** Refund Eligibility · **Last updated:** August 5, 2026

Confirmed billing errors (duplicate charges, incorrect proration, mis-applied promo codes, incorrectly billed add-ons) are eligible for a goodwill refund or account credit of **up to $75.00 per account per rolling 12-month period**. This is a cumulative ceiling across the trailing 12 months, not a per-dispute allowance — check the account's credit history before quoting an amount. Requests that would exceed the cumulative $75.00 limit require Tier 2 Billing Specialist approval; do not promise an amount above $75.00 without that approval.

This article covers **general** billing errors only. Charges related to a confirmed service outage are handled under a separate policy — see KB-1004.
