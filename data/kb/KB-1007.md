### KB-1007 — Repeat Disputes & Fraud Review
**Category:** Escalation Rules · **Last updated:** August 20, 2026

If an account has filed a second billing dispute within 90 days of a previous *resolved* dispute, the new dispute must be handled by a Tier 2 Billing Specialist, even if the amount in question is small. The same applies if fraud indicators are present, such as a disputed charge tied to a SIM or device not registered on the account. Front-line agents should gather details and set expectations with the customer but should not issue a credit themselves in these cases.
