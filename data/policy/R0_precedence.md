# Nimbus Wireless — Billing Dispute & Refund Policy
**Internal reference for NimbusCare Customer Support — Billing Team**
Document ID: BIL-POL-014 · Version 6.2 · Effective: August 3, 2026
Owner: Billing Operations · Applies to: Postpaid mobile and home internet accounts

> This document governs how NimbusCare agents evaluate and resolve customer-initiated billing disputes and refund/credit requests. Rules are numbered for reference during live calls and chats. Where more than one rule appears to apply, the more specific rule for the situation governs; agents should not blend two rules into a single calculation.
